Trips become albums

The app finds your trips and creates albums named by place and time, like “Lake Garda, August 2026.”

Countries, and cities if you like

Each photo goes into an album for its country — and for its city, too, if you like. Places you visit often get names you choose.

Screenshots and documents

Tickets, invoices, recipes, and orders are recognized and sorted into albums of their own.

Your albums stay yours

Existing albums are added to, never duplicated. You review every suggestion before anything happens, and you can undo everything.

Your Travel Atlas

A world map of every country you’ve taken photos in. Ready to share as an image.

Pay once

Your first 10 albums are free. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited albums. No subscription.

Private by design

  • All analysis runs on your iPhone.
  • No photo is ever uploaded. No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • A photo leaves the app only when you share it yourself.
  • For place names, iOS sends a sample of photo locations, roughly one per area (only coordinates and the language for place names), to Apple’s map service; you can turn this off in the app.
  • Your originals are never deleted, changed, or copied.
