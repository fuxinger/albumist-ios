Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. {{APP_NAME}} never uploads your photos. All analysis runs on your iPhone. There’s no {{APP_NAME}} server and no account. A photo leaves the app only when you share it yourself. For place names, iOS sends a sample of photo locations, roughly one per area (only coordinates and the language for place names), to Apple’s map service; you can turn this off in the app under Settings › Privacy › “Refine places online”. See the privacy policy for details.

Does the app delete or change my photos?

No. {{APP_NAME}} only creates albums and folders in the Photos app and adds photos to them. The photos themselves stay as they are. The only exception: if you add sample photos to try the app, {{APP_NAME}} can remove them for you; iOS asks you to confirm the deletion. You can undo every change in the app.

Why does the first analysis take so long?

The first time, the app looks at your whole library. With many thousands of photos, this can take a while; trips and places come first. The app finishes the rest in the background, usually overnight while your iPhone charges.

The app doesn’t see all my photos.

You probably shared only selected photos. Open Settings › Apps › {{APP_NAME}} › Photos and choose “Full Access”.

I use iCloud Photos. Does {{APP_NAME}} work?

Yes. For trips and places, the information already on your iPhone is enough. The app downloads originals from iCloud only for screenshots and documents, and only if you turn this on in the app’s settings; by default, it then uses Wi-Fi only. Videos are never downloaded.

A place or trip was detected incorrectly.

Places come from the GPS data in your photos. If a photo has none, the app can’t tell where it was taken. Before anything is created, you can reject or rename any suggestion, and split or merge trips.

I bought the full version. How do I restore it?

Open the app’s settings and tap “Restore Purchase”. Make sure you’re signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

All of the app’s data is stored on your iPhone, and a small copy of your settings and decisions is included in your iPhone backup. In the app’s settings, “Rebuild Analysis” deletes the stored analysis. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Albums in the Photos app stay until you delete them yourself or undo them in the app.
